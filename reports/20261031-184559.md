### 3. Reflection AI unveils Beam 501B open-weight model under Apache 2.0 license
`reflection/beam/release`

Reflection AI introduced Beam, its first open-weight model, featuring 501 billion total and 23 billion active parameters. Beam is slated for release under an Apache 2.0 license with quantized FP8 and NVFP4 numerics.

Evidence:
- Reflection AI introduced Beam, its first open-weight model, featuring 501 billion total and 23 billion active parameters.  
  Source: <https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.introducing-beam>  
  > Reflection introduces Beam, its first open-weight model: a sparse Mixture-of-Experts with 501 billion total and 23 billion active parameters, aimed at coding, reasoning, and agentic workloads.
- Beam is slated for release under an Apache 2.0 license with quantized FP8 and NVFP4 numerics.  
  Source: <https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.introducing-beam>  
  > Now in the final stages of red-teaming, Beam will be released under an Apache 2.0 license this month, along with quantized FP8 and NVFP4 numerics for efficient deployment.
